Step one

The bearing block

This is the object we'll draw. Drag to orbit it and get a feel for its shape — the base, the central boss, and the through-bore. The small wireframe camera is the , the eye whose view we'll be casting onto glass.

The reference planes

Three glass planes now surround the object: the below, the behind, and the to the right. Each one will catch a flat image of the object.

Lines of sight

Move the Observer to each principal view. Watch the faint lines of sight run from its eye to every corner of the object, and that view's flat 2D outline appear on its glass plane. Pick a view below to send the Observer there.

Looking from the front, parallel projectors strike the Vertical Plane (VP) at right angles to form the Front View.

Looking from above, parallel projectors strike the Horizontal Plane (HP) at right angles to form the Top View.

Looking from the side, parallel projectors strike the Profile Plane (PP) at right angles to form the Side View.

The complete glass box

All three views cast at once — Top on the HP, Front on the VP, Side on the PP — each plane carrying the object's exact flat outline. This is the full "glass box" the object sits inside.

Unfolding into 2D

Now watch the box unfold: the HP swings down and the PP swings out to the right, laying all three views flat around the fixed VP — the classic first-angle multiview drawing. Use the rail's Unfold/Fold toggle to replay it.
