About this object

The blue arrow marks the front. Whoever makes the drawing decides which face that is; every other view is named from it.

Why only four directions?

A view is taken from exactly one of the four. Turn the object even slightly off one and you get a picture that is nice to look at and impossible to measure. That is what means.

Drawing rules

The side you look from and the side it is drawn on are opposite. That is what does, and it is the thing most people get backwards at first.

  1. Front view first — everything else is measured from it.
  2. Top view below it, lines dropped straight down.
  3. Side view beside it, heights carried across.
Construction notes

Press Draw next under the drawing. The object turns to face the direction each view is taken from as that view is drawn, so the two halves of the screen always agree.

Building lines — thin, and only there to line the views up. They fade once they have done their job.
Outline — thick and solid. The edges you could really see from this direction.
Hidden edges — short dashes for edges buried inside the material. Without them, nobody can tell there is a hole there.
Centre lines — long chain through anything round. It says "this is round, and this is the middle".

Sizes go on last, in the — along the line each one measures, and given once. The Dimensions button at the top left hides and shows them at any time, on the drawing and on the solid alike.
