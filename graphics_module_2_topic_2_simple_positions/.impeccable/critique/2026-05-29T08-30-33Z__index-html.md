## Overall Impression

The sim has moved from "strong with a few real gaps" to "genuinely polished within its restrained intent." The three P1s from the first pass are closed: the palette collision, the missing inline help, and the token-contract drift. The biggest remaining opportunity is not visual at all, it is the **silent downstream-wipe** when a learner edits an early step after building later ones, and a **dedicated responsive/mobile pass**, which has not been exercised this session.

## What's Working

1. **Functional color now reads unambiguously.** Teal HP, amber VP, blue chrome: three jobs, three hues, verified to clear WCAG non-text contrast on warm paper. The classic engineering teal/amber pair carries the drawing and survives a dim projector.
2. **The struggling learner is now scaffolded end to end.** Empty-state orients the eye, the orbit hint reveals the one hidden interaction, terms are defined the first time they appear, and hints add rather than repeat. This is exactly the persona PRODUCT.md optimizes for.
3. **The system documents itself honestly.** DESIGN.md and the sidecar now describe what actually ships, so the next module inherits a true contract instead of an aspirational one.

## Priority Issues

- **[P2] Editing an early step silently discards later work.** Changing the solid or its pose after labelling/projecting/flattening tears those layers down. It is announced to the live region, but there is no confirm or undo, so a learner who nudges a slider at Step 2 can lose a Step 5 result without intending to. *Fix:* a lightweight confirm when downstream work exists, or a non-destructive "your projections will update" reflow instead of a teardown. *Suggested command:* `harden`.

- **[P2] No dedicated responsive/mobile pass this session.** The layout stacks below 768px and the advisory banner appears, but the wizard-on-top + viewport behavior, touch target spacing, and the fixed-position term popover have not been exercised on small screens. *Fix:* verify and tune the breakpoint behavior. *Suggested command:* `adapt` (or `audit` for a11y/responsive checks).

- **[P3] Orbit-hint dismiss target is 28px.** Below the 44px touch minimum the rest of the UI honors. Mitigated by drag-anywhere and auto-dismiss, but inconsistent. *Fix:* enlarge the hit area to 44px without growing the chip visually (padding/`::before`). *Suggested command:* `polish`.

- **[P3] Vertex labels can still crowd on dense solids.** The outward nudge helps, but a hexagonal prism's twelve labels may overlap at some orbit angles. *Fix:* simple screen-space de-clutter, or fade labels on faces pointing away. *Suggested command:* `polish`.

## Persona Red Flags

**Priya (struggling first-year):** Her prior blockers are resolved, she now learns she can rotate (orbit hint), sees what the empty viewport is for, and can look up `HP`, `orthographic projection`, and `ground line` in place. Residual risk: if she tweaks the solid after reaching Step 5, her flattened drawing vanishes with only a spoken announcement to explain it.

**Mr. Rao (instructor on a projector):** Teal/amber/blue are now separable at the back of the room, and the legibility fonts help. Residual: the reference grids are still faint, and small secondary text remains a stretch at distance.

**Keyboard-only learner:** Still a strength, visible focus halo, arrow-key sliders, operable nav, and the new term buttons are focusable with `aria-describedby`. No new red flags.

## Minor Observations

- Reference grids remain very faint; a touch more contrast would help projector and low-vision use.
- The step rail on the far right is unconventional for a progress spine (intentional, user-requested).
- The downstream-wipe announcement is good; pairing it with a visible inline note ("Later steps cleared") would help sighted users who miss the live region.

## Questions to Consider

- Should editing an early step preserve later work and reflow it, rather than tearing it down?
- Is there a single contrast bump (grids, secondary text) that would make the whole thing read from the back row?
- Does the far-right rail still earn its place, now that the card carries all the guidance?
